What exactly is Rackyweb Global Media?+
Rackyweb Global Media is Africa's premier business, technology, and media platform — combining startup intelligence, AI-powered business tools, a digital marketplace, and a community of 48,000+ entrepreneurs and investors from 190+ countries.
Who is the platform built for?+
We primarily serve entrepreneurs, startup founders, investors, freelancers, and corporate innovators across Africa and the global African diaspora. However, our content, tools, and marketplace serve business builders everywhere in the world.
How do I access the AI tools?+
AI tools are accessible on all paid plans. The Starter plan includes our Business Plan Generator. The Growth plan unlocks the full suite — including the Pitch Deck Builder, Market Intelligence Engine, and BusinessBot Assistant.
Can I sell products on the marketplace?+
Yes! Any Growth or Enterprise member can list and sell digital products — templates, tools, courses, and business kits — on our marketplace and earn up to 85% revenue share.
Where is Rackyweb Global Media based?+
We are headquartered in Mbaitoli LGA, Imo State, Nigeria — with a global community spanning 190+ countries. We operate fully online, making our platform and events accessible from anywhere in the world.
